## Privacy Policy
_Last updated: September 22, 2026_

Acidity does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or user data.

**Data storage**: All data is stored locally using the browser's storage API. No data is sent to Octalwise or any third party.

**Third parties**: Acidity does not contact any external servers for any purposes, including advertising, analytics, or tracking.

**Contact**: [acidity@octalwise.com](mailto:acidity@octalwise.com)
